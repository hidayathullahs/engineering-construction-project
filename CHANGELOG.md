### Added & Transformed
- **Brand Elevation to BUILD MY DREAM**:
  - Unified brand architecture under **BUILD MY DREAM** with core promise *"From Vision to Reality."*
  - Re-anchored copy, messaging, and emotional journey around luxury residential dream-home architecture and engineering.
- **Signature "YOUR DREAM HOME, BUILT STEP BY STEP" Experience**:

  - Maintained interactive Three.js structural model viewer with real-time orbit controls, layer filtering, and FEA load stress simulation.

  - Added residential budget brackets (`Below ₹25L`, `₹25L–₹50L`, `₹50L–₹1Cr`, `₹1Cr+`, `Prefer not to say`).

- **8-Stage Turnkey Dream-Home Process**:
  - Configured pipeline: `DISCOVER`, `CONSULT`, `DESIGN`, `APPROVE`, `ENGINEER`, `BUILD`, `INSPECT`, `HANDOVER`.
- **Comprehensive 14-Part Documentation Suite**:
  - Updated all files in `docs/01_PROJECT_BRIEF.md` through `docs/14_TASK_TRACKER.md`.
