# 02_PRD.md — Product Requirements Document (BUILD MY DREAM)

## 1. Product Objectives
- Create an immediate high-end architectural impression.
- Clearly present the **BUILD MY DREAM** promise: *"From Vision to Reality."*
- Showcase the complete 16-stage homebuilding journey via the 300-frame time-lapse canvas scrubber.
- Provide interactive 3D BIM structural exploration without compromising performance.
- Convert visitors into qualified homeowner inquiries via the accessible dream-home estimator form.

## 2. Target Personas
- **Dream-Home Builder**: Looking to design and build a custom home from scratch on a purchased plot.
- **Luxury Villa Owner**: Seeking contemporary architectural estates with cantilevered designs, pools, and high ceilings.
- **Renovation Client**: Looking to dramatically modernize an existing residence with steel structures and glass pavilions.

## 3. Core Feature Requirements
- **Hero Cinematic**: Responsive mouse parallax, live studio & site sensors HUD widget, and clear CTAs (`START YOUR DREAM →`, `EXPLORE PROJECTS →`).
- **Signature Scrubber**: 300-frame 60fps HTML5 canvas engine rendering the 16 dream-home milestones.
- **Projects Directory**: Filterable by `ALL`, `MODERN HOMES`, `LUXURY HOMES`, `VILLAS`, `CONTEMPORARY`, `RENOVATION`.
- **Services Catalog**: 8 dedicated residential practices.
- **Process Pipeline**: 8-stage turnkey workflow (`DISCOVER` to `HANDOVER`).
- **Estimator & RFP Form**: Complete client validation with budget brackets (`Below ₹25L`, `₹25L–₹50L`, `₹50L–₹1Cr`, `₹1Cr+`, `Prefer not to say`).
